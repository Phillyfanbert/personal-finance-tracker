Financial context

Your data and privacy

Where your information is kept

Everything you enter is stored in this app's own database, run by Supabase. The app itself is served by Cloudflare. Nothing is stored on the phone or computer you use beyond the small items listed further down.

Who can see it

Other people using this app cannot see your money. The database checks who is asking on every single request and returns only your own rows, so this holds even if the app itself has a bug.

The person who set this app up can. Running it requires a key that reads the whole database, and they hold it. That is normal for a household app, but you should know it rather than assume otherwise: treat what you record here as visible to them.

What never leaves

Your transactions, balances, account names and notes are never sent to an outside company. When you ask the app a question about your spending, the answer is written by a model running on a computer in this household, not by a cloud service.

What does get sent out

Three things leave, and none of them include who you are or what you spend:

  • Stock and fund symbols you track, sent to a price service to fetch the current price.
  • The names of subscription services you record, used to search public pricing pages for a cheaper plan. The service name only, never your account or what you pay.
  • Your browser loads three shared code libraries from a public code network (jsDelivr) when the page opens, which is how it sees your IP address. That is the same for any site using a public library.

What is stored on your device

Your sign-in, so you stay signed in, and a handful of small display preferences: which tabs you had open, which breakdown you last chose, and whether you picked the light or dark look. There are no cookies and no advertising or tracking code of any kind.

Getting a copy, or deleting it

The two buttons above this one download everything you have recorded, as a file saved to your own device. To have your information deleted, ask the person who set this app up.

The code is public, your data is not

How this app works is published openly so anyone can check these claims. What you record is not part of that and is never published.

Terms of use

What this app is

A private record of money you enter yourself, shared by a small household. It is not a bank and it never moves, holds or transfers real money. Changing a balance here changes a note about your money, not the money.

It only knows what you tell it

Every total, chart and answer is worked out from what has been recorded. If something was not entered, it is not counted, and figures will read low without saying so. Checking the numbers against your real statements is on you.

Not financial advice

Nothing here is advice from a licensed professional. The app shows you your own numbers and general, widely published information. It will not tell you what to buy or sell, and it does not know your full circumstances. For decisions that matter, talk to someone qualified.

Prices and written summaries

Share prices come from an outside service and can be delayed, missing or wrong, and some kinds of investment have no live price at all. Written recaps and answers are produced by a computer from figures the app calculated, and are labelled where they appear. Treat both as a helpful guide, not as a record of fact.

Availability, and keeping your own copy

This runs on free services and, for some features, on a computer in this household. When that machine is off or the internet is down, parts of the app stop working and prices stop updating. There is no guarantee it will be available, and no promise that data cannot be lost. Use the download buttons in Profile now and then so you have your own copy.

Using it

Accounts are for members of this household, given out by the person who runs it, and can be withdrawn by them. Record your own money, not anyone else's, and do not try to reach another person's records.

Changes

These terms can change as the app does. There is no notification when they do, so re-read them if it matters to you. How your information is handled is covered separately, under "Your data and privacy".
